Glintvial Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10 · Applies to the Glintvial mobile app (iOS and Android), bundle ID com.cngstudio.glintvial.

1. Who we are

Glintvial is published by CngStudio, operated by Yaren Ünlü, Vatan Mahallesi, Süleyman Binek Caddesi 91/C, Serdivan, Sakarya, Türkiye. Contact for all privacy questions: cngstudiodev@gmail.com.

For the limited data described below that is processed through ad and purchase software built into the app, we act as the party that decides to include that software. Google and RevenueCat process that data under their own privacy policies (linked below).

2. Short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no name or e-mail address is collected by the app.
  • We run no server of our own for the app and use no analytics service of our own.
  • Your game progress stays on your device. We cannot see it.
  • The app shows ads (Google AdMob) and offers in-app purchases (processed by RevenueCat, Apple and Google). These services process some technical data, described below.
  • The app is for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children.

3. Data stored only on your device

Level progress, stars, daily puzzle records, streaks, settings (sound, vibration, theme, language, reminder time), hint balance and purchase ledger, counters used to space out ads and offers, and the last known purchase status. This data is stored locally and is not sent to us. To delete it, use Settings > Reset progress or uninstall the app. Because it exists only on your device, it cannot be restored if the device is lost, reset, or the app is deleted.

5. Ads, consent and your choices

  • Consent form. Where the law requires consent for ads (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before requesting ads. Ads are not requested if consent that is required has not been obtained.
  • Personalised ads only with consent. We ask for personalised ads only where you have agreed. If you decline, or no consent is available for your region, the app requests non-personalised ads (based on context such as the app and general location, not on your advertising profile). The game works the same and no feature depends on your choice.
  • iOS tracking permission. On iOS the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before using the advertising identifier. If you say no, ads are not personalised.
  • Change your mind. Use Settings > Privacy and ad choices (shown where available) to reopen the consent form, and your device settings to reset or limit the advertising identifier.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience aged 13 and over.

6. Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. There is no remote push service and no push token. The permission is requested only when you switch the reminder on and can be revoked in device settings.

7. Children

Glintvial is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. In some countries a higher age applies for consenting to certain data processing (such as personalised advertising); if you are below that age in your country, please do not agree to personalised ads without a parent or guardian. If you believe a child under 13 uses the app, contact us at cngstudiodev@gmail.com; because we hold no accounts, the main step is removing app data on the device and, where possible, asking Google or RevenueCat to delete data linked to the device's identifiers.

8. Legal bases (where GDPR / UK GDPR applies)

  • Consent: ad-related storage and access on your device, personalised ads (you can withdraw at any time as described above).
  • Contract: processing needed to complete and restore your purchases.
  • Legitimate interests: limited technical operation, fraud prevention and security of purchase and ad services, balanced against your rights.

Where Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK) applies, we rely on its corresponding processing conditions, including explicit consent where required, and this policy also serves as the information notice (aydınlatma metni) for the data described here. Turkish version: Gizlilik Politikası.

9. International transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process data in countries other than yours, including outside the EEA, the UK and Türkiye, using the safeguards described in their own policies. We do not transfer data ourselves because we do not receive it.

10. Retention

On-device data stays until you reset progress or uninstall. Data held by Google, RevenueCat, Apple and Google Play is kept according to their policies. We do not keep a copy.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK or US state laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, port your data, and complain to a regulator. Because we do not hold your data, requests about ad or purchase data are best sent to Google (privacy tools) and RevenueCat; you can also write to cngstudiodev@gmail.com and we will help route or answer the request within the time the law requires. We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for automated decisions with legal effect.

Supervisory authorities: in Türkiye the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK); in the EEA and UK, your national data protection authority.

12. Security

The app does not store your data on our servers. Ad and purchase services use encrypted connections. No method is perfectly secure.

13. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and, where required, ask for consent again. Previous versions are available on request.
